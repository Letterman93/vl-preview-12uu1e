About

Founder-led. Defense-trained.

Visulogic is led by Zak Nizam, an Air Force veteran who has worked as a data engineer, data management analyst and project manager on mission-critical defense technology programs, including AI work in imagery analysis and language models.

That background covers the full life of a software project: gathering requirements from the people who will use it, designing databases and data pipelines, preparing and labeling data for machine learning, integrating AI models into working systems, and testing them against real-world data. It also covers the work that keeps a project on track: planning schedules and budgets, coordinating with stakeholders, managing risk, documenting decisions and meeting strict security and quality standards.

Visulogic applies the same standards to businesses of every size. Clients work directly with the engineer building their system, and projects are planned and measured using Lean process-improvement methods.

Questions

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Custom web applications, data pipelines and integrations, workflow automation and technical reviews. Projects that involve messy or scattered data are a particular strength.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. Visulogic is a good fit for small teams that have outgrown spreadsheets but do not need an enterprise system.

How is a project priced?

After a free discovery call, you receive a written plan with scope and price. Well-defined projects can be quoted at a fixed price.

Do you maintain what you build?

Yes. Every project ends with documentation so your team can run it on its own, and optional monthly support plans cover fixes, updates and small improvements. Larger new features are scoped as their own projects.

Do you use AI?

Yes. Our experience includes AI systems for government programs, such as imagery analysis and language models that process documents, and we build AI features into client systems where they add value. AI tools also speed up our development, and all code is reviewed and tested before release.

What products has Visulogic built?

Our flagship product is LandWeather, a land intelligence platform that turns public flood, elevation, wetland and storm records into sourced property reports. It is in development in Florida, with plans to expand across the United States.

Tell us about your project. We respond within one business day.
