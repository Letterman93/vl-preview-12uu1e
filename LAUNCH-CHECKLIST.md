# Launch checklist

Items to finish before sharing visulogic.net publicly.

- [ ] Connect the contact form to a form-processing service (for example Formspree): set the form `action` and `method="POST"`, and test a submission end to end. The privacy policy already describes a form-processing service.
- [ ] Confirm contact@visulogic.net receives mail (the privacy policy promises access, correction and deletion requests through it, answered within 30 days).
- [ ] Buy and connect the visulogic.net domain; update the footer and links if the address changes.
- [ ] Once Visulogic LLC is formed, add "Visulogic LLC, Florida" to the footer and update "Who we are" in privacy.html.
- [ ] Remove `noindex` (index.html, privacy.html) and robots.txt blocking when ready to appear in search.
- [ ] If analytics are ever added, choose a cookie-free option and update privacy.html first.
- [ ] Before LandWeather takes payments: its own terms of service, refund policy and privacy policy, reviewed by an attorney.
