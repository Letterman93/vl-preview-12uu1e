Effective November 2, 2026

In short: this website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking. If you contact us, we use your details only to reply and to keep a record of our conversation. We never sell your information. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

This website and Visulogic are operated by Zak Nizam in Florida, United States ("Visulogic," "we," "us"). You can reach us about this policy at contact@visulogic.net.

Information we collect

Information you send us. When you use our contact form or email us, we receive the information you provide: your name and email address, and, if you choose to include them, your company, a description of your project and your timeline.

Contact form delivery. Messages sent through our contact form are delivered to us by a form-processing service, which receives and stores the submission so it can pass it on to us.

Information collected automatically. We do not use cookies, web analytics, advertising tools, tracking pixels or social media plugins, and we do not collect information about your activity on this site. Our fonts, images and scripts are served from this website itself, not from third-party services.

Our hosting provider. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. GitHub handles that information under its own privacy statement. We do not use these logs.

How we use your information

  • To read and reply to your message.
  • To discuss and plan a project you have asked us about.
  • To keep a record of our business correspondence.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not add you to a mailing list or send you marketing email unless you ask us to.

How we share your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only:

  • with the service providers that host this website, deliver our contact form messages and provide our email, so they can serve this site and deliver and store messages for us;
  • when required by law, such as in response to a valid legal request; or
  • with your permission.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages for as long as we need them to respond to you and to keep reasonable business records. If you ask us to delete your information, we will do so unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices: access, correction and deletion

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Email contact@visulogic.net with your request, and we will respond within 30 days.

Do Not Track and other tracking

This site does not track visitors over time or across other websites, so it does not respond differently to "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signals. We do not allow third parties to collect personal information about your online activities over time and across different websites through this site.
